YOU MIGHT ASK

Frequently asked questions.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?

Yes — our team arrives with professional-grade supplies and equipment as standard, so there's nothing you need to prepare in advance.

How quickly can I get a quote?

Send your details through the contact form or the quick-quote box in the hero, and we'll come back with a free, transparent quote — usually within one business day.

Do you offer one-off cleans or only recurring contracts?

Both. Regular weekly or bi-weekly visits, one-off deep cleans, and move-in / move-out jobs are all available, scheduled around you.

Can cleaning companies subcontract staff through you?

Yes — we provide trained cleaning staff and subcontracting support for other cleaning companies who need reliable extra coverage.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Stockholm and serve the surrounding areas. Get in touch with your address and we'll confirm coverage.
